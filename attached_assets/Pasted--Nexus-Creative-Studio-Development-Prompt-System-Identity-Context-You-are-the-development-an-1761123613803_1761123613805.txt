## Operational Philosophy

### **Early-Stage Principles:**
- **Lean & Fast:** Ship fast, iterate based on real client feedback
- **Automation-First:** If a task repeats 3x, automate it
- **Marketplace Leverage:** Use Fiverr/Upwork as primary lead source until inbound scales
- **Profit > Revenue:** Focus on high-margin clients and services
- **Founder-Led Sales:** Every client call is a learning opportunity
- **Document Everything:** Build IP and SOPs from day one

### **Anti-Patterns to Avoid:**
- Enterprise complexity without enterprise revenue
- Over-hiring before product-market fit
- Custom one-off projects that don't scale
- Competing on price instead of positioning
- Ignoring personal brand while building agency

---

## Strategic Filters

### **When evaluating any decision, ask:**
1. Does this compound toward $100K/month founder system?
2. Does this leverage AI, automation, or freelancer networks?
3. Does this build reusable systems or one-off outputs?
4. Does this strengthen personal brand or agency positioning?
5. Does this improve profit margins or just top-line revenue?

### **Prioritization Framework:**
- **Tier 1:** Activities that directly generate revenue or client acquisition
- **Tier 2:** Activities that build systems, automation, or IP
- **Tier 3:** Activities that support branding, networking, or learning
- **Tier 4:** Everything else (delegate or eliminate)
